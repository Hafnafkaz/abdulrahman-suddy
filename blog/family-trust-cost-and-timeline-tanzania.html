How Much Does a Family Trust Cost in Tanzania, and How Long Does It Take?

The government fee is the smallest part of the cost. The real costs are drafting, transferring assets and running the trust properly every year.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 6 min read
Quick answerSetting up a family trust in Tanzania involves RITA government fees (an official schedule from 2023 lists the incorporation fee alongside fees for annual returns, changes and land consents), advocate fees for drafting the deed and handling registration, costs of transferring assets such as stamp duty and land registration, and ongoing annual compliance. Timelines depend mainly on preparing documents and recommendation letters and on RITA processing. Ask for a written quote.

The four cost buckets

  1. Government fees. RITA charges set fees for incorporation and later services, such as annual returns, notifications of changes and consent for land transactions. Check the current schedule with RITA.
  2. Professional fees. Advocates and advisers charge for structuring, drafting and filing. These vary with complexity.
  3. Transfer costs. Stamp duty, land registration charges and any tax on gains when assets move into the trust.
  4. Running costs. Accounts, filings, tax returns and trustee time each year.

What drives the price up

  • Many assets, especially land in several places
  • A family business with other shareholders
  • Beneficiaries or trustees abroad
  • Complex family situations

A realistic timeline

  1. Planning and structure: family discussions and advice.
  2. Drafting the deed: a few rounds of review.
  3. Gathering documents: recommendation letters often take longest.
  4. RITA processing and queries.
  5. Transferring assets: land registration can take time.

How to get a fair quote

  • Share a full asset list and your goals.
  • Ask what is included: drafting, filing, transfers, first-year compliance.
  • Ask for government fees to be shown separately.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the RITA fee to register a trust?

RITA charges a set government fee for incorporation under its published schedule, separate from professional fees. Confirm the current amount with RITA.

How long does it take to register a trust in Tanzania?

It depends on how quickly documents and recommendation letters are gathered and on RITA processing. Transferring land into the trust can take longer.

Are there ongoing costs?

Yes. Annual returns, notifications, accounts, tax filings and trustee time all continue after registration.
