Family Trusts for Tanzanians in the Diaspora: Protecting Assets Back Home

Building at home while living abroad is a leap of faith. Too often, property is held by a relative "for now", and "for now" becomes a dispute.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerTanzanians abroad can use a trust to hold property in Tanzania through trustees who manage it locally under clear written rules. For registered trusts, published guidance requires resident trustees (a sole trustee must be ordinarily resident, and at least two resident where there are two or more). Land rules depend on nationality under the Land Act, and income from trusts abroad can be taxable for resident beneficiaries. Take advice before structuring.

The common problem

Many in the diaspora register land or houses in a relative's name for convenience. Years later, the relative dies, sells, or claims it. A trust replaces trust in a person with trust in a structure.

How a diaspora trust works

  1. You settle the trust and transfer Tanzanian assets to it.
  2. Local trustees manage, collect rent and maintain the property.
  3. The deed sets reporting, accounts and limits on what trustees can do without your consent.
  4. You and your family benefit as beneficiaries.

Key rules to check

  • Trustee residency for registered trusts.
  • Land and nationality. The Land Act restricts land holding by non-citizens and by bodies majority-controlled by non-citizens. Status matters.
  • Tax. The trust may be taxed in Tanzania on local income; check tax where you live too.
  • Beneficial ownership. Records of non-resident beneficiaries and owners may be required.

Practical tips

  • Require quarterly reports and annual accounts in the deed.
  • Use a separate trust bank account with dual signatures.
  • Include an independent trustee, not only relatives.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Tanzanian living abroad set up a trust in Tanzania?

Yes. You can settle a trust over Tanzanian assets, with trustees who meet the residency rules for registered trusts.

Can trustees live outside Tanzania?

For registered trusts, published guidance requires a sole trustee to be ordinarily resident in Tanzania, and at least two resident where there are two or more.

Can non-citizens hold land through a trust?

Nationality rules in the Land Act can restrict land holding. Take advice before transferring land.
