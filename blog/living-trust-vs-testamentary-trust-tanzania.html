Living Trust vs Testamentary Trust in Tanzania: Revocable or Irrevocable?

Do you want your trust working today, or waiting until your will takes effect? And do you want to keep the power to undo it?

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 19 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerA living trust is created during your lifetime through a trust deed and takes effect immediately; it is often revocable, so you can change or end it. A testamentary trust is created in your will and starts only on your death; it is generally irrevocable once it takes effect, unless the beneficiaries agree to changes. Living trusts can keep assets outside probate; testamentary trusts are simpler to set up but rely on the will being valid.

Living (inter vivos) trusts

  • Created by a trust deed while you are alive.
  • Works from day one: you can watch how trustees manage it.
  • Assets transferred in are generally outside your estate on death.
  • Often revocable, depending on the deed.

Testamentary trusts

  • Created by your will.
  • Starts only after death and after the estate is administered.
  • Generally irrevocable once in effect, unless beneficiaries agree to change it.
  • Depends entirely on the will surviving any challenge.

Revocable or irrevocable?

A revocable trust lets you change your mind. That flexibility can be attractive, but it can also weaken the separation between you and the assets. An irrevocable trust gives stronger separation and certainty, at the cost of control. The deed decides which you have, so decide deliberately.

How to choose

If you want...Consider
To see the trust working and test the trusteesLiving trust
Something simple to protect young children if you dieTestamentary trust in your will
Strong separation and continuity for a family businessIrrevocable living trust
Flexibility while your family situation is changingRevocable living trust

Frequently asked questions

What is a testamentary trust in Tanzania?

A trust created in a will that takes effect after the testator dies. It names trustees, beneficiaries, the property and the trustees' powers.

Is a living trust revocable in Tanzania?

It can be, if the deed reserves a power of revocation. Living trusts are commonly revocable, but you can make one irrevocable.

Which trust avoids probate?

A living trust, for assets properly transferred into it during your lifetime. A testamentary trust is created through the will, so the estate still goes through probate first.
