What Rights Do Beneficiaries Have in a Tanzanian Trust?

Beneficiaries do not own the trust assets, but they are the reason the trust exists. They have rights, and trustees should respect them.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 6 min read
Quick answerBeneficiaries of a Tanzanian trust are entitled to the benefits the trust deed gives them, to have trustees act loyally and in their best interests, to fair treatment according to the deed, and generally to reasonable information and accounts about their interest. If trustees breach their duties, beneficiaries can take action, including seeking accounts or compensation through the courts. The deed should spell out information and reporting rights to avoid disputes.

Core rights

  • To benefit as the deed provides.
  • To loyalty. Trustees act for beneficiaries, not themselves.
  • To fair treatment under the deed's terms.
  • To information about the trust and their interest.
  • To accounts showing how the trust is managed.
  • To a remedy if trustees breach their duties.

Why the deed should say it clearly

In the 2024 Mberesero case, a foundation could not force relatives to account because the duty to account was never clearly established. Beneficiaries' information rights should be written into the deed, not assumed.

What beneficiaries cannot do

  • Demand assets outside the deed's terms.
  • Control trustees' discretion where the deed gives it to trustees.
  • Treat trust assets as personal property.

Good practice for trustees

  1. Send annual accounts to adult beneficiaries.
  2. Hold an annual family meeting.
  3. Explain decisions, even when saying no.

Frequently asked questions

Can beneficiaries see the trust accounts?

Beneficiaries generally have a right to reasonable information and accounts about their interest. Write this into the deed to avoid argument.

Can a beneficiary remove a trustee?

Only if the deed gives that power or a court orders it for good reason such as serious breach.

What can a beneficiary do if a trustee steals or mismanages?

Seek accounts, compensation or removal through the courts, and report registered trusts' issues to the Administrator-General.
