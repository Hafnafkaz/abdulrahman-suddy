Registered Trustees vs NGO vs Company in Tanzania: Which for a Charity or Foundation?

Families who want to give back often ask the same question: should the foundation be a trust, an NGO or a company? The answer depends on what it will do.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerIn Tanzania, Registered Trustees are trustees incorporated under the Trustees' Incorporation Act through RITA, suited to holding property for religious, educational, social, charitable or kinship purposes. NGOs register under NGO law and companies under the Companies Act with BRELA. Since 2019 amendments, the Trustees' Incorporation Act excludes certain bodies, including NGOs, companies, trade unions, political parties, sports associations, cooperatives and VICOBA, so you must choose one route.

The exclusions

Following the 2019 amendments, the Act lists bodies that cannot register as trusts, such as NGOs, companies, trade unions, political parties, sports associations, cooperative societies and VICOBA groups, plus any body the Minister declares.

Which should a family foundation choose?

  • Holding endowment assets such as land, buildings or investments that fund a cause: Registered Trustees.
  • Running projects with staff and donor programmes: often an NGO.
  • Earning income from trading: a company, possibly owned by the trust.

Some families combine them: Registered Trustees own the assets and a company runs commercial activity.

Governance from day one

Whatever the vehicle, write duties, reporting and accounts clearly. The 2024 Mberesero case involved a family foundation that could not enforce duties against relatives because they were never clearly created. See the case.

Frequently asked questions

Can an NGO register as a trust in Tanzania?

No. The Trustees' Incorporation Act, as amended in 2019, excludes NGOs and several other bodies, which must register under their own laws.

What are Registered Trustees?

Trustees incorporated as a body corporate under the Trustees' Incorporation Act, with perpetual succession and a name that includes "Registered Trustees".

Should a family foundation be a trust or an NGO?

If its main role is holding assets for a purpose, Registered Trustees often fit. If it will run programmes, an NGO may suit better. Take advice on your specific plans.
