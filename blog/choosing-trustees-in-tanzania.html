Choosing Trustees in Tanzania: Who Can Serve and Who Should

A trust is only as good as the people running it. Choosing trustees is the single most important decision after drafting the deed.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 19 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerFor a registered trust in Tanzania, trustees must meet the Trustees' Incorporation Act's rules: published guidance notes that a sole trustee must be ordinarily resident in Tanzania and that where there are two or more, at least two must be resident, and people with convictions for fraud or dishonesty, or who are bankrupt, are restricted. Beyond the law, choose people who are honest, organised and willing, appoint at least two, mix family with an independent person, and always name successors.

Legal eligibility

  • Residency. Guidance on the Act states that a sole trustee must be ordinarily resident in Tanzania, and that with two or more trustees at least two must be resident.
  • Disqualifications. People convicted of offences involving fraud or dishonesty, or who are bankrupt or in certain arrangements with creditors, cannot act in management.
  • Documents. Each trustee provides identification, photographs, a CV and contact details for registration.

Many families combine the two: one or two trusted relatives plus an independent person, such as an advocate, accountant or respected family friend.

What makes a good trustee

  1. Integrity above all.
  2. Organisation. Trustees keep records and meet deadlines.
  3. Judgement to weigh competing needs fairly.
  4. Availability and willingness to do the work for years.
  5. Acceptability to the family, so decisions are respected.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Appointing one person who is also the only beneficiary, with no one to check them.
  • Appointing someone because of seniority alone.
  • Forgetting successors, so the trust stalls when a trustee dies.
  • Appointing people abroad without checking the residency rule.

Write the trustee rules into the deed

The deed should say how trustees are appointed, removed and replaced, how they decide, whether they are paid and how conflicts are handled. See trust deed clauses.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner be a trustee in Tanzania?

For registered trusts, published guidance on the Act requires a sole trustee to be ordinarily resident in Tanzania, and at least two resident trustees where there are two or more. Check the current rules with RITA.

How many trustees should a family trust have?

At least two, with named successors. Two or three trustees, mixing family members and an independent person, is a common balance.

Can a beneficiary also be a trustee?

Often yes, but there should be at least one other trustee to provide a check, and the deed should deal with conflicts of interest.
