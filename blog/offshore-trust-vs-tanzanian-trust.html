Offshore Trust vs Tanzanian Trust: Which Is Right for Your Family?

Offshore trusts sound sophisticated. For most Tanzanian families with Tanzanian assets, a well-run local trust is simpler, cheaper and easier to defend.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerAn offshore trust is set up under the laws of another country, often one with developed trust law, while a Tanzanian trust is governed by Tanzanian law and commonly incorporated with RITA. Offshore trusts can suit families with international assets or beneficiaries, but distributions from a non-resident trust are generally included in a Tanzanian resident beneficiary's income, local assets remain subject to Tanzanian law, and disclosure obligations still apply. For mainly Tanzanian assets, a local trust is usually the practical choice.

When offshore can make sense

  • Significant assets outside Tanzania
  • Family members living in several countries
  • Need for a mature trust law framework for complex arrangements

What offshore does not do

  • It does not take Tanzanian land outside Tanzanian law.
  • It does not remove disclosure duties, including beneficial ownership.
  • It is not a way to hide assets. Arrangements designed to hide or evade can be challenged.

A practical rule

Put Tanzanian assets in a Tanzanian structure. Consider offshore only for genuinely international assets, with advice in both jurisdictions.

Frequently asked questions

Should I set up an offshore trust as a Tanzanian?

Usually only if you have significant international assets or family abroad. For Tanzanian assets, a local trust is normally simpler and cheaper.

Are distributions from offshore trusts taxed in Tanzania?

Tanzanian commentary notes that distributions from non-resident trusts are included in the resident beneficiary's income under the Income Tax Act.

Can an offshore trust own land in Tanzania?

Land ownership by foreign or foreign-controlled bodies is restricted under the Land Act. Take advice before attempting it.
