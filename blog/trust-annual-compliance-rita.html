Running a Registered Trust in Tanzania: Annual Returns and RITA Compliance

Many trusts are set up carefully and then forgotten. Compliance is what keeps the certificate valid and the trustees protected.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerAfter incorporation, a registered trust in Tanzania must file an annual return of trustees, notify changes to trustees, the trust deed, name or postal address within the time limits set by the Act and RITA (published guidance gives one month), keep proper accounts that the Administrator-General may inspect, obtain consent before acquiring or disposing of land, keep beneficial ownership information current, and meet tax obligations. Failure can lead to penalties or revocation.

Your compliance calendar

ObligationWhen
Annual return of trusteesEvery year, within the period set after each 12 months
Change of trusteesNotify promptly, within the period set
Change of trust deedNotify within the period set
Change of name or addressNotify within the period set
Land acquisition or disposalGet consent before the transaction
AccountsKeep continuously; produce when required
Tax returnsAs TRA requires

Published guidance on the Act gives one month for annual returns and change notifications. Confirm current periods and forms with RITA.

Records to keep

  • Certificate of Incorporation and trust deed
  • Minutes of trustee meetings
  • Register of trust property
  • Bank statements and accounts
  • Beneficial ownership details and changes

What can go wrong

The Administrator-General can revoke incorporation in certain cases, such as where the certificate was obtained by fraud, the trust has ceased to own property, or certificate conditions are not met. Trustees also remain personally accountable for trust property.

Make it easy

  1. Appoint one trustee or an adviser as compliance lead.
  2. Set calendar reminders for each deadline.
  3. Hold one formal trustee meeting a year at minimum.

Frequently asked questions

Do registered trusts file annual returns in Tanzania?

Yes. Registered trusts file an annual return of trustees with RITA.

How quickly must I report a change of trustees?

Published guidance on the Trustees' Incorporation Act gives one month. Confirm the current period with RITA.

Can RITA cancel a trust's registration?

Yes. The Administrator-General can revoke incorporation in certain cases, such as fraud in obtaining the certificate or failure to meet its conditions.
