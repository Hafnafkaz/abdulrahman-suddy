Family Trusts in Kenya vs Tanzania: What East African Families Should Know

East African families often hold assets on both sides of the border. Trust advice from Nairobi does not automatically work in Dar es Salaam.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerKenya and Tanzania both recognise trusts, but the rules differ. Kenya amended its tax laws in 2021 to give registered family trusts specific exemptions on certain transfers and income. In Tanzania, trusts are commonly incorporated with RITA under the Trustees' Incorporation Act, and we found no confirmed equivalent family trust tax exemption. Families with assets in both countries usually need separate structures or careful cross-border advice.

  • Map which assets sit in which country.

  • Check tax residence of trustees and beneficiaries.

The takeaway

Learn from Kenya's approach, but plan under Tanzanian law for Tanzanian assets. Read how trusts are taxed in Tanzania.

Frequently asked questions

Do Kenyan family trust tax exemptions apply in Tanzania?

No. Kenya's exemptions come from Kenyan law. Tanzanian assets follow Tanzanian rules, and we found no confirmed equivalent exemption.

Can one trust hold assets in Kenya and Tanzania?

It is possible in principle but complex. Many families use separate local structures with coordinated deeds. Take advice in both countries.

Where is it easier to set up a family trust?

It depends on your assets and goals. Kenya has specific tax incentives for registered family trusts; Tanzania offers incorporation through RITA. Compare with advisers in both.
