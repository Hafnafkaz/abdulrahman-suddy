Family Trust vs Will in Tanzania: Which Do You Need?

A will speaks when you die. A trust starts working while you are alive. That single difference changes almost everything.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 19 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerA will says who inherits your estate after death and must usually go through the court process of probate or administration. A family trust holds assets now, through trustees, under a private trust deed, so assets already in the trust are generally outside your personal estate. A will is essential for everyone. A trust adds continuity, privacy and protection for children or a family business. Most families with significant assets use both.

When a will alone is enough

If your assets are simple, your heirs are adults, and there is no family business to keep together, a well-drafted will may be all you need for now.

When a trust adds real value

  • Young children, or a dependant who cannot manage money.
  • A family business or property portfolio that should stay together.
  • Children from more than one relationship.
  • A wish for privacy and continuity.

Why you still need a will with a trust

Anything you have not transferred into the trust still passes under your will or the intestacy rules. A short "pour-over" provision can direct remaining assets into the trust.

A Tanzanian reminder

Wills can be set aside. In a widely reported case, the High Court declared a prominent businessman's will invalid over its form, his capacity and the exclusion of children. Read the Mengi will case.

Frequently asked questions

Is a family trust better than a will in Tanzania?

They do different jobs. A will is essential for everyone; a trust adds ongoing management, privacy and protection. Families with significant assets usually use both.

Does a family trust avoid probate in Tanzania?

Assets properly transferred into the trust during your lifetime are generally outside your personal estate, so they do not go through probate. Assets left outside still do.

Do I still need a will if I have a trust?

Yes. A will deals with everything not in the trust and can direct leftover assets into it.
