How Are Trusts Taxed in Tanzania? Income, Distributions and Transfers

Trusts are often sold as tax tools. In Tanzania they are mainly protection and succession tools, with tax rules you must plan around, not ignore.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 8 min read
Quick answerUnder the Income Tax Act, a trust is generally treated as a separate taxpayer on its own income. Tanzanian legal commentary notes that distributions from a resident trust are exempt in the beneficiaries' hands, while distributions from a non-resident trust must be included in the beneficiary's income. Transferring assets into a trust is a transfer like any other, so stamp duty and capital gains tax can apply. No Tanzanian source we reviewed confirms a special tax exemption for family trusts, unlike Kenya. Confirm with TRA or a tax adviser.

The trust as a taxpayer

A trust that earns rent, dividends, interest or business income is generally taxed on that income itself. Trustees must register for tax where required and file returns.

Distributions to beneficiaries

  • Resident trust. Commentary from Tanzanian law firms notes that distributions are exempt for the beneficiary, because the income was taxed in the trust.
  • Non-resident trust. Distributions from a trust outside Tanzania are included in the resident beneficiary's income under the Income Tax Act.

Transfers into the trust

  • Stamp duty can apply to transfers of land and shares, including transfers without payment.
  • Capital gains can arise if the asset has grown in value since you acquired it, because a transfer is a disposal.
  • Land transfers also involve registration charges and, in some cases, approvals.

What about special exemptions?

Kenya introduced specific tax exemptions for registered family trusts in 2021. We found no confirmed equivalent in Tanzanian law. One Tanzanian guide refers to exemptions without citing provisions. Until a statutory provision is confirmed, plan on the basis that ordinary rules apply. Read Kenya vs Tanzania family trusts.

Plan, do not guess

  1. List the assets you plan to transfer and their cost and current value.
  2. Ask a tax adviser to calculate stamp duty and any gain.
  3. Consider transferring income-producing assets first.
  4. Keep the trust's books from day one.

Frequently asked questions

Do trusts pay tax in Tanzania?

Yes. A trust is generally treated as a separate taxpayer on income it earns.

Are trust distributions taxable in Tanzania?

Tanzanian commentary notes that distributions from a resident trust are exempt for beneficiaries, while distributions from a non-resident trust are included in the beneficiary's income.

Is there a tax exemption for family trusts in Tanzania?

We found no confirmed Tanzanian provision granting family trusts special exemptions, unlike Kenya. Confirm with TRA or a tax adviser before relying on any exemption.

Is stamp duty payable when I move property into a trust?

Stamp duty generally applies to transfers of land and shares, including transfers without payment. Get the calculation done before you sign.
