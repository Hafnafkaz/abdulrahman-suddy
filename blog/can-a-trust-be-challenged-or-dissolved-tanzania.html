Can a Trust Be Challenged or Dissolved in Tanzania?

A trust is designed to last, but it is not untouchable. Knowing how trusts end or get challenged is the best way to build one that survives.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerIn Tanzania, a trust can end by revocation if the settlor reserved that power, by the beneficiaries' consent, when its term expires, or when its purpose fails. It can be challenged and declared invalid if it was induced by fraud, duress, undue influence or mistake. For registered trusts, the Administrator-General can revoke incorporation, for example if the certificate was obtained by fraud, the trust no longer owns property or it breaches its certificate conditions.

How trusts end

  • Revocation by the settlor, if the deed reserves the power, or by will.
  • Beneficiary consent, in appropriate cases.
  • Expiry of the trust period.
  • Failure of purpose, for example if the trust property is destroyed.

How trusts are challenged

  • Fraud, duress or undue influence in creating the trust.
  • Mistake about what was being created.
  • Lack of capacity of the settlor.
  • Sham arrangements where the settlor kept full control and the trust was never real.
  • Breach of trust claims against trustees for mismanagement.

Revocation of registration

For registered trusts, the Administrator-General can cancel incorporation in defined circumstances. If that happens, the trustees must return the certificate.

Build a trust that lasts

  1. Set it up early, while healthy, with independent advice.
  2. Transfer assets properly and stop treating them as personal.
  3. Write clear duties and decision rules.
  4. Explain the plan to the family.
  5. Keep compliance up to date.

Frequently asked questions

Can a family member challenge a trust in Tanzania?

Yes, on grounds such as fraud, undue influence, lack of capacity or breach of trust. Well-documented trusts set up early are harder to challenge.

Can I cancel my family trust?

Only if the deed gives you a power of revocation, or with the beneficiaries' agreement in appropriate cases.

Can RITA deregister a trust?

The Administrator-General can revoke incorporation in defined cases, such as fraud in obtaining the certificate or failure to meet its conditions.
