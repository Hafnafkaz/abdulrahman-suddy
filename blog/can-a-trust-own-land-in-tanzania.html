Can a Trust Own Land in Tanzania? Rules, Consents and Registration

Land is most families' biggest asset, and the one most often fought over. Here is how a trust can hold it properly.

Abdulrahman SuddyBusiness & Financial Advisory · Last updated: 19 November 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answerYes. Once incorporated, registered trustees can hold, acquire, transfer and lease land in their corporate name, subject to the conditions in their certificate, which can limit how much land they hold and how it is used. In practice, registered trusts obtain the Administrator-General's written consent before acquiring or disposing of land, and every transfer must be registered at the land registry. Nationality rules under the Land Act also apply.

The legal power

Under the Trustees' Incorporation Act, a registered trust has perpetual succession and power to hold and acquire land, and to transfer, convey, assign or lease it under its common seal, subject to the conditions in its certificate. The Administrator-General can restrict the amount of land a trust may hold and the uses it can be put to.

Consent before buying or selling

RITA's published requirements and its fee schedule include consent to acquire or dispose of land. Plan for this step before signing any sale agreement.

Registering the transfer

  1. Confirm the land's title and any encumbrances.
  2. Obtain the required consents.
  3. Execute the transfer under the trust's seal.
  4. Pay stamp duty and any other charges.
  5. Register the transfer at the land registry so the title shows the trust.

Nationality matters

Under the Land Act, organisations with majority non-citizen membership face restrictions on holding land, and courts have considered whether land rights can pass to non-citizen heirs. If any trustees, beneficiaries or settlors are non-citizens, take advice before transferring land. Read trusts for the diaspora.

Unregistered family land

Much family land is held informally or under customary rights. Formalising title first makes it far easier to place in a trust and far harder to dispute later. See trusts and customary law.

Frequently asked questions

Can a registered trust buy land in Tanzania?

Yes, subject to its certificate conditions and, in practice, the Administrator-General's written consent. The purchase must be registered at the land registry.

Does land in a trust go through probate?

Land properly transferred into a trust is generally no longer part of the settlor's personal estate, so it does not pass through probate.

Can non-citizens benefit from a trust holding land?

Nationality rules in the Land Act can restrict land holding by non-citizens. Take advice before structuring a trust with non-citizen trustees or beneficiaries.
