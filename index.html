About

A little about me.

I'm a business and financial advisory professional focused on helping entrepreneurs, families, investors and organisations make better decisions about capital, ownership, growth and long-term wealth.

My work sits at the intersection of investment banking, corporate finance, mergers & acquisitions, wealth structuring, estate planning and international business structuring. I help clients understand complex decisions, structure opportunities properly, and move from ideas to executable transactions — connecting the financial numbers to the operational and strategic realities behind a business.

  • Buy-side and sell-side M&A, valuation, capital raising and deal structuring
  • Estate planning, succession and family wealth structuring
  • Investment advisory, Islamic finance and cross-border structuring
Build well. Structure intelligently. Protect what you create. Transfer it deliberately.

What I do

Six areas where I help.

Often treated separately — but connected. A business that raises capital needs to understand its valuation; a founder preparing an exit needs to understand succession. I work across the whole chain.

Investment Banking & Corporate Finance

Valuation, capital raising, financial modelling, transaction advisory and corporate finance strategy — so owners and investors understand the economics before capital changes hands.

Mergers & Acquisitions

Buy-side and sell-side: preparing a business for sale, valuation, due diligence, deal structuring and negotiation support — making M&A accessible to the African private-business market.

Estate Planning & Wealth Structuring

Succession, family governance, trust structures, asset protection and intergenerational transfer — helping families preserve wealth, ownership and objectives across generations.

Investment & Capital Advisory

Investment analysis, opportunity assessment, capital allocation, Islamic finance and Sukuk — clear thinking about the relationship between risk, return, liquidity, control and long-term value.

International & Offshore Structuring

Cross-border ownership, holding-company and offshore structures, and international expansion — designed around legitimate commercial, investment and succession purposes.

Business Strategy & Financial Management

Corporate strategy, financial reporting, controls, budgeting and management reporting — moving growing businesses from founder-driven to structured, measurable and professionally managed.

In action

On stage and in the room.

Speaking, teaching and working with founders, teams and cohorts on the decisions that move a business forward.
